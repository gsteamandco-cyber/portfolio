Customers List

Add customers here first; they appear in the Sales Register dropdown. Billed / Received / Outstanding are calculated from Sales Invoices.

Sales Register – Receivables

Edit cells directly. Customer name, credit days, due date, CGST/SGST (customer GSTIN state = 29) or IGST, balance, status and days overdue calculate automatically. Items (description, HSN, qty, unit, rate) are added per invoice on the Sales Invoice page; when items exist, Taxable Value is the sum of the items. Data saves in this browser.

Purchases & Expenses (incl. GST)

Enter bill, vendor, category, type, taxable value, GST %, credit days and amount paid. GST (input credit), total, due date, balance and status are calculated. Choose CGST+SGST or IGST per bill.

Outstanding Payables – Vendor-wise Ageing

Vendors are picked up automatically from Purchases & Expenses.

Bank Statement

Same layout as the Excel BANK STATEMENT: DATE, REFERENCE NO., RECEIVED FROM, AMOUNT, PAID TO, AMOUNT, BALANCE (opening balance + received − paid). Enter the Invoice No. / Bill No. in Reference No.: Received From / Paid To and the amount fill in automatically and the money is applied to that invoice/bill first. Or pick a customer / vendor directly (applied to their oldest open items), and the receipt/payment is applied to their oldest open invoices/bills, reducing Outstanding Receivables / Payables everywhere (Dashboard, Sales Register, Purchases). Import CSV columns: date, reference no., received from, amount, paid to, amount.
